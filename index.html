    Twelve short chapters explain how the platform works and what it means for your dealership.

      Customers review a dealership on many different websites. Keeping up means checking each one, writing a reply in the right tone, and doing it before an unhappy customer feels ignored. A3 Review Responder brings this work together, and the A3 team runs it for you.

      AI suggests the wording. A person decides what is published.

      What your dealership gets

      One record of your reviews

      Reviews from the platforms where you are listed, with your full history collected.

      Prompt notice

      An email when a recent review arrives, with a link that opens it.

      Replies in your voice

      Suggested responses based on what the customer wrote and how your dealership already writes.

      Follow-up on low ratings

      A waiting reply to a 1 or 2 star review is escalated to the A3 leads after 48 hours.

      Stages 5 and 7 need a person. Nothing reaches a review site without them.

      What you see along the way

      You get the new review email at stage 3, without waiting for a reply to be written. If your own team answers the review first, the tool picks this up on platforms that show replies, and the suggested reply is withdrawn.

      How the sites are read

      Google

      Read through the Google Business Profile connection once your listing is linked. A complete read runs about once a day to catch removed reviews. If the connection fails for 12 hours, a backup source takes over.

      The other six

      Read once a day from your public pages, using a third-party web collection service.

      Your history and recent reviews

      Your full history is collected so your figures are complete. Only recent reviews trigger action, so you won't get a flood of emails when you join.

      Suggested responses are written by Claude, Anthropic's AI model, under fixed instructions. They are a starting point for the person who approves them.

      Addresses the customer

      Opens with the customer's first name. If no name is given, it doesn't invent one.

      Stays with the facts

      Refers to what the customer actually wrote. It is told not to invent facts or promise refunds, discounts or callbacks.

      Follows your voice

      Up to five of your past replies guide its tone, greeting and length. It is told not to copy their sentences.

      Stays short

      Two to four sentences, in the same language as the review.

      Examples

      Customer review

      ★★★★★

      Second car I've bought here. Ask for Danny, no pressure at all.

      Suggested response

      Sarah, thank you for coming back to us. We're glad Danny made it easy, and we'll pass your kind words along. Enjoy the new car.

      Customer review

      ★

      Waited three hours for an oil change and nobody told me why.

      Suggested response

      Tom, we're sorry about the long wait and that nobody kept you informed. That isn't what we want for our customers. Please get in touch with our service team so we can look into what happened.

      These examples are made up to show the style. They aren't real customers or real replies.

      Reviews with no written comment

      A review with stars but no text gets a fixed template, without AI. A 1 or 2 star rating gets an apology and an invitation to get in touch, a 3 star rating gets thanks and an invitation, and a 4 or 5 star rating gets a thank-you.

      1 and 2 star reviews

      The AI can't know what happened at your dealership, so the person approving should add what it can't. A strong reply includes a specific detail and a real contact, doesn't argue in public, doesn't name an employee, and goes out quickly.

      Automatic posting is switched off. Approving a reply doesn't publish it by itself.

      A3 checks drafts first as standard, and you never have to approve anything. Approval emails go to A3, not to your dealership. Approve links in email need no sign-in, and each link is unguessable and works once.

      How drafts stay visible

      1. When the draft is madeA3's approver is emailed.
      2. After 2 daysThe approver gets a daily reminder, worst rating first.
      3. After 48 hours, for 1 and 2 star reviewsThe A3 leads are emailed every day until the reply is cleared.

      Safeguards

      • One decision counts. If two people click Approve, only the first takes effect.
      • No double replies. If your team has answered on a platform that shows replies, the draft is withdrawn and approving it is refused.
      • Discarding leaves a record. An approved reply taken out of Ready to post is marked with who did it and why.

      Replies on other platforms are marked as posted by the person who pasted them, and the tool doesn't verify this. If you answer a review on Carfax, CarGurus or Edmunds yourself, please tell A3, since the tool can't see those replies.

      What the links open

      The New review button opens that customer's review where the platform allows. On Google it goes to the exact review once the tool has its link, and otherwise to your listing. On sites with one page of many reviews, it scrolls to the review in browsers that support this.

      What stays with A3

      Approval emails, reminders, overdue escalations and recurring complaint alerts go to the A3 team and not to your dealership. Monthly reports aren't emailed. You read them in your portal.

      With any questions, please contact your A3 team directly rather than replying to a notification email.

      The portal is one page for your dealership. A login sees only the dealerships linked to it, and an owner with several can switch between them.

      Accounts aren't self-service. If anyone could sign up and choose a dealership, a stranger could see its reviews, so A3 sets up each client.

      What A3 will ask you for

      • Your dealership's name and the review sites where you are listed
      • Access to your Google Business Profile, so that listing can be read
      • The email addresses that should get new review emails, and who needs a login

      There is no self-service password reset. If you lose your password, your A3 team can issue a new one.

      How reviews are counted

      Labelled copies count once

      Cars.com labels reviews copied from DealerRater, and those are linked. A customer who separately reviews on two sites counts twice.

      Removed Google reviews stop counting

      The daily complete read finds them, and they return automatically if Google shows them again. Removal from other sites isn't detected.

      Google's own numbers

      Your rating and total come from Google on each read, so they can differ from the number of reviews tracked.

      No guessed ratings

      Carfax and Carfax Service don't publish stars per review. The tool shows none and never treats that as a low rating.

      How access is protected

      • Google connection. The key for reading your listing is encrypted with AES-256-GCM.
      • Bot protection. The public sign-in page uses Cloudflare Turnstile.
      • Activity records. Administrative changes and sign-ins are logged.

      Backups and monitoring

      Nightly backups

      Review data is backed up every night and kept for 14 days. The database copy is restored into a test database each night to prove that it works.

      Monitoring

      A watchdog checks the system every 5 minutes, restarts stopped services after two failed checks, and alerts the A3 team.

      Can anything be posted without approval?

      No. Automatic posting is off, and a reply is only published after a person approves it and someone at A3 posts it.

      Do I have to approve replies myself?

      No. A3 checks drafts as standard. If you have a portal login, you can also approve, edit or skip waiting drafts there.

      Does every review get an AI reply?

      Recent Google reviews get a draft automatically, up to five per run. For other platforms, the A3 team requests one from the dashboard when needed.

      What if I already replied to a review?

      On Google, DealerRater and Cars.com, the tool sees your reply at its next read and withdraws the draft. On other platforms it can't see replies, so please tell A3.

      Why do some reviews have no star rating?

      Carfax and Carfax Service don't publish a rating for each review, so the tool leaves it blank.

      Why does my total differ from the reviews tracked?

      Your rating and total come directly from Google, while the tracked number covers each platform collected.

      Will I get emails about old reviews?

      No. Your history is collected, but new review emails only cover recent reviews.

      Can I change who gets the emails?

      Yes. Send your A3 team the addresses you want to use.

      Can other people at my dealership use the portal?

      Yes. Ask A3 to add a login for each person. Each login sees only the dealerships linked to it.
